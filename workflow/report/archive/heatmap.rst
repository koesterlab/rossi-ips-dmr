Comparison of DMR methylation differences between the experiments and the base experiment, aggregated per transcript and gene region (found by `ChIPseeker <https://bioconductor.org/packages/release/bioc/vignettes/ChIPseeker/inst/doc/ChIPseeker.html>`_). Rows are transcript/gene-region combinations, columns are experiments; where a transcript/gene-region has several DMRs within an experiment, the mean methylation difference is shown, and gene regions without any DMR in an experiment are shown as 0.

Methylation at known pluripotency and germ layer biomarker CpGs, one panel per biomarker group (psc, endoderm, mesoderm, ectoderm, and endomeso for markers shared between endoderm and mesoderm). Methylation is direction-corrected, so that high values always indicate similarity to the targeted cell fate: for markers defined as hypomethylated the complement of the methylation level is shown. Within each panel, points are split by whether the germ layer is the targeted cell fate of the biomarker or not, and colored by germ layer. The subtitle reports the summed direction-corrected methylation per germ layer.

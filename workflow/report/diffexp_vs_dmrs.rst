Per-layer scatter plot of differential gene expression against DMR mean methylation difference, one point per gene. Point size and opacity encode the combined DMR/differential-expression q-value; a slider restricts the view to genes below a chosen q-value threshold. The line is a linear regression fit, and labeled points mark genes from the validation gene list. The subtitle reports the number of plotted genes and their Pearson correlation with a q-value of 0.05.
